# AcmeFlow Cancellation Policy

Workspace owners can cancel a subscription from **Settings > Billing > Plan**.
Cancellation stops the next renewal; it does not remove access immediately.
The workspace remains available until the end of the current paid billing
period.

Monthly and annual plans follow the same self-service cancellation flow. A
workspace owner who cannot access billing settings should contact support from
the email address on the workspace. Support can explain the policy, but it
cannot confirm a customer's current cancellation state without account access.

Cancellation and a refund are separate requests. Review the Refund Policy for
the 30-calendar-day refund window.
