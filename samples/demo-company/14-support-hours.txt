AcmeFlow support hours

Self-service documentation and the AcmeFlow support assistant are available at
all times. Human support is staffed Monday through Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 UTC,
excluding published AcmeFlow holidays.

Urgent security reports should be submitted through the verified support
channel even outside staffed hours. The team reviews them in the next staffed
window. Account-specific status questions are not answered by guessing; they
are routed for human review.
