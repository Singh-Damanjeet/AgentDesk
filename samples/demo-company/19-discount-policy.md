# AcmeFlow Discount Policy

Promotional discounts apply only to the plan, term, and period shown in the
offer. Unless the offer says otherwise, a discount applies to the first billing
period and does not renew automatically.

Only one promotional code can be applied to a checkout. Codes cannot be
exchanged for cash and are not transferable. Existing annual contracts follow
their contract terms.

Questions about whether a specific workspace has a discount require account
access and should be sent to a human teammate.
