# AcmeFlow Payment Failures

When a recurring payment fails, AcmeFlow shows a billing alert and retries the
charge according to the payment schedule for the workspace. The billing owner
should verify the card or payment method, billing address, and expiration date
in **Settings > Billing**.

Do not send a full card number or security code to support. If the payment
method is correct but the charge still fails, ask the billing owner to contact
the payment provider and then request human review from AcmeFlow.

The assistant cannot see retry attempts, a bank response, or the current
payment state for an individual workspace.
