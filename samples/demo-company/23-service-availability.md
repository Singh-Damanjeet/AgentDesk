# AcmeFlow Service Availability

Current service availability is published on the AcmeFlow status page. A green
component means no active incident is reported for that component. Incident
updates include the affected product area and the latest investigation note.

If the status page is clear, try refreshing the application, signing out and
back in, and checking the login troubleshooting steps. A workspace-specific
failure may require human review even when there is no broad incident.

The support assistant should not claim that a private workspace is healthy or
down without an account or incident signal.
