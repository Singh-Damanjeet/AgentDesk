# AcmeFlow Escalation Policy

Support escalates requests when a customer needs account-specific information,
reports a security concern, describes a service outage, or cannot resolve an
issue using the published guidance. Human review is also appropriate when the
assistant has insufficient evidence for a reliable answer.

An escalation creates or updates a support ticket and preserves the customer
conversation. The assistant should explain what it can and cannot verify. It
must not invent an order status, refund status, account setting, or internal
case result.

Urgent security and availability incidents are prioritized for the human team.
Normal policy questions can remain in the self-service flow when the relevant
published document provides enough evidence.
