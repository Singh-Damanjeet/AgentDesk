# AcmeFlow Customer Onboarding

Start with a workspace name, invite the initial members, and choose the plan
that matches the team's size. Set a billing owner, enable two-factor
authentication, and review the member permissions before importing work.

A practical first week is:

1. Create one shared workflow.
2. Invite a small pilot group.
3. Add the team's support and security guidance.
4. Review activity and update roles.

The support team can explain onboarding steps. It cannot perform setup actions
or inspect a customer's workspace from a general chat request.
