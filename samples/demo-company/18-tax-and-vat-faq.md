# AcmeFlow Tax and VAT FAQ

AcmeFlow calculates applicable tax from the billing address and regional tax
rules available at checkout. A valid business tax ID may change the tax shown
when it is entered and verified during billing.

Invoices show the billing details used for the charge. If the tax details on a
particular invoice look incorrect, contact support from the workspace account
and include the invoice number without sharing payment credentials.

The support assistant can explain this published guidance. It cannot inspect a
customer's tax profile or provide tax advice for a particular jurisdiction.
