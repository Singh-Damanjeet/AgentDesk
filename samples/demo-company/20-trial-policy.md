# AcmeFlow Trial Policy

New workspaces may be eligible for a 14-day Growth trial. The trial starts when
the workspace is created and ends at the time shown in the billing screen.
Some partner or contract offers may have different terms.

A workspace owner can choose a plan before the trial ends. If no plan is
selected, the workspace returns to the available free experience or becomes
read-only according to the offer shown at signup.

The assistant cannot confirm an individual trial's end time or conversion
status without account access.
