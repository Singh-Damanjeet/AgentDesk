# AcmeFlow Two Factor Authentication

Members can enable two-factor authentication from **Profile > Security**.
AcmeFlow supports authenticator-app codes and recovery codes. Save recovery
codes somewhere secure and do not share them with anyone.

If an authenticator device is lost, use a saved recovery code. A workspace owner
can remove a member's access while the member recovers the account. Support may
help with ownership verification, but it cannot bypass two-factor
authentication from a chat request alone.

For a question about a specific login or security event, request human review.
