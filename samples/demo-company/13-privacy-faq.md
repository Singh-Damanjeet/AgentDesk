# AcmeFlow Privacy FAQ

AcmeFlow uses workspace data to provide the service, maintain security, and
support product functionality described in the customer agreement. Workspace
owners can request a copy of workspace data through the Data Export process.

Personal data requests should come from an authorized workspace owner. The
support assistant can explain the published process, but it does not inspect
private records or confirm the status of an individual request.

For retention, deletion, and legal-request questions, review the Data Deletion
Policy and the agreement applicable to the workspace.
