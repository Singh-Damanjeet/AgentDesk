# AcmeFlow Subscription Plans

AcmeFlow offers three plans for teams:

- **Starter** supports up to 10 members and includes shared workflows.
- **Growth** supports up to 50 members and adds approval routing and audit
  history.
- **Scale** supports unlimited members and adds priority support and advanced
  security controls.

Prices are shown in the workspace billing screen and can change by region or
contract. The billing screen is the source of truth for a customer's current
price. Support can explain plan features but cannot see private billing data.

Plan changes normally take effect at the next billing boundary unless the
workspace owner is shown an immediate-change option during checkout.
